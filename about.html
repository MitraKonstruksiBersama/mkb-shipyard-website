About Our Company

Established on October 11, 2022, in the heart of the Batam maritime hub, PT Mitra Konstruksi Bersama was founded on a vision of engineering excellence. As a fully licensed entity under the BP Batam Free Trade Zone Authority, we have rapidly emerged as a premier partner for global ship owners seeking high-performance marine solutions.

Our Vision

To be the premier international benchmark for shipbuilding excellence, recognized globally for uncompromising quality, professional integrity, and world-class maritime engineering.

Our Mission

  • Customer Focus: Dedicating ourselves to client needs through integrated, continuously improving services to achieve maximum satisfaction.
  • Continuous Improvement: Continuously advancing our synergy, operational performance, and workmanship.
  • Modernization: Building a robust, modern, and updated fleet while prioritizing efficiency and safety at every step.

Our Motto

SPEED

We prioritize synchronized efficiency to meet the most demanding maritime schedules. By integrating advanced project management with rapid execution, we ensure your fleet returns to operation on time, every time—with zero compromise on safety or structural integrity.

QUALITY

Quality is the cornerstone of our maritime legacy. From the selection of premium-grade steel to the application of advanced BKI-standard fabrication techniques, we ensure that every hull and system we deliver maintains peak structural integrity—empowering our clients to navigate the world’s oceans with absolute confidence and safety.
